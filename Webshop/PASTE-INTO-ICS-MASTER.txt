If your project uses src/ (codeunit, page, table, …), paste the Webshop folder here:

  ZVG-Nonpa/
    app.json
    src/
      codeunit/
      page/
      Webshop/     ← here (your layout)

Control add-in paths must then be:
  src/Webshop/scripts/productViewer.js
  src/Webshop/scripts/startup.js
  src/Webshop/styles/productViewer.css

If you instead paste Webshop next to app.json (not inside src), use paths without src/:
  Webshop/scripts/productViewer.js

Then Download Symbols and F5.
