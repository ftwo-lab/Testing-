Copy this folder into your AL app (ZVG-Nonpa / ICS Master).

Do NOT copy PIM-Product-Family as one nested folder.
Merge src into your existing src folders:

  PIM-Product-Family/src/enum/*            ->  your-app/src/enum/
  PIM-Product-Family/src/table/*           ->  your-app/src/table/
  PIM-Product-Family/src/tableextension/*  ->  your-app/src/tableextension/   (create folder if missing)
  PIM-Product-Family/src/codeunit/*        ->  your-app/src/codeunit/
  PIM-Product-Family/src/page/*            ->  your-app/src/page/
  PIM-Product-Family/src/pageextension/*   ->  your-app/src/pageextension/

app.json idRanges must include 50120 to 50136.

Then publish (F5).

Zip of this folder: PIM-Product-Family.zip at the repo root.
